Chronology

Places, days and times come from the text. Points sit on the modern positions of the places Wells names; where he gives only an area (the Byfleet pine woods, the sea fight off the Essex coast, the brother’s cross-country leg toward Colchester) the point or line is approximate. The Martian line is a reading of the battle reports in Book I, chapters XIV–XVII, not a single machine’s track. Text: Project Gutenberg.
